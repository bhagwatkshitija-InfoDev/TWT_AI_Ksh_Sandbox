# German Formatting Checklist

Apply every rule below to the document. Quote the exact offending text and its location.

## 1. Noun capitalization
Capitalize every German common and proper noun — not just sentence-initial words.
This also covers nominalized verbs/adjectives used as nouns.

- Incorrect: Der hund lief schnell durch den park.
- Correct: Der Hund lief schnell durch den Park.
- Incorrect: Das lesen macht spaß.
- Correct: Das Lesen macht Spaß.

## 2. Quotation marks
Use German low-high quotes „…" as the default, or guillemets »…« as the alternate
style. Do not use English " " or ' ' quotes.

- Incorrect: Er sagte "Guten Tag".
- Correct: Er sagte „Guten Tag". (or: Er sagte »Guten Tag«.)

Flag mixed styles within the same document (some quotes „…", others " ") as
inconsistent even when each instance is individually a valid style elsewhere.

## 3. ß vs ss — Eszett rules
Use ß after a long vowel or diphthong; use ss after a short vowel.

- Incorrect (ss used after long vowel, should be ß): die Strasse, ich weiss nicht
- Correct: die Straße, ich weiß nicht
- Incorrect (ß used after short vowel, should be ss): der Fluß, das Faß
- Correct: der Fluss, das Fass

Note: Swiss Standard German (de-CH) replaces ß with ss entirely. If the document is
explicitly labeled as Swiss German, skip this rule and mark it ➖ NOT APPLICABLE.

---

## 4. Compound-word spacing — "Deppenleerzeichen" (additional)
German compound nouns are written as one word, not split with a space.

- Incorrect: Handy Ladegerät, Software Entwicklung
- Correct: Handyladegerät, Softwareentwicklung
(Hyphenate only where needed for readability, e.g. with proper nouns/acronyms:
E-Mail-Adresse.)

## 5. Decimal comma vs. decimal point (additional)
German uses a comma as the decimal separator and a period (or thin space) as the
thousands separator — the opposite of English convention.

- Incorrect: The price is 1,234.56 EUR.
- Correct: Der Preis beträgt 1.234,56 EUR.

## 6. Date format (additional)
German dates are written DD.MM.YYYY, not MM/DD/YYYY or unlabeled YYYY-MM-DD.

- Incorrect: 03/14/2026
- Correct: 14.03.2026
